01 At a glance

  • We collect the minimum needed to run your account and save your learning progress.
  • No ads, no third-party analytics, no tracking SDKs, and no advertising identifier.
  • We never sell or rent your personal data.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app.

SkillForge AI is an AI-skills learning app published by Auralis Tech. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean Auralis Tech.

02 What we collect

Account information

DataWhy we need it
Email addressTo create your account, sign you in, and send password resets
PasswordStored only as a salted hash — we never see or store your plain password

If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and basic profile information from Google instead of a password.

Profile and preferences

Collected during onboarding, and editable later in your profile:

  • Display name, and an optional public username
  • Your profession or role, experience level, and learning goals
  • Daily XP goal, target study minutes, and reminder time
  • Time zone — used to calculate streaks and daily goals correctly
  • Whether you have opted in to leaderboards

Learning and progress

  • Lessons and exercises completed, and the answers you gave
  • XP, level, streak, hearts, gems, quests, achievements, and league placement
  • Questions you got wrong, so the app can schedule spaced-repetition reviews

AI feature content

When you use the AI Tutor, AI Mentor, AI Quiz Generator, or AI Practice Coach, the message you write — plus context such as the current lesson and a summary of your progress — is sent to our AI provider to generate a response. Your email address and password are never included.

Purchases

Google Play processes all payments. We receive only a purchase token and subscription status in order to unlock premium features. We never receive or store your card, bank, or billing details.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera input, call or SMS data, list of installed apps, or advertising identifier. The app contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, and no crash-reporting SDK.

03 How we use it

We use your data only to:

  • Create and secure your account
  • Save and sync your learning progress across devices
  • Personalise your learning path and AI feedback
  • Run streaks, quests, achievements, and leagues
  • Send the reminder notifications you have enabled
  • Unlock premium features you have purchased
  • Diagnose problems and prevent abuse of the service

We do not use your data for advertising or for profiling unrelated to learning, and we do not sell it.

04 Who we share it with

Only the service providers needed to operate the app:

Our AI provider processes your messages to return a response and does not use them to train their models. We may also disclose data where required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

Where your data lives. Account and progress data is stored on servers in Sydney, Australia (AWS ap-southeast-2). AI requests may be processed in the United States. If you use the app from elsewhere, your data will be transferred to and processed in these locations.

05 Leaderboards & friends

These features are opt-in, controlled by a setting in your profile.

  • While opted in, other users can find you by username or display name, and can see your display name, username, avatar, level, streak, and weekly XP.
  • Your email address is never shown to other users.
  • Turning the setting off removes you from search results and public leaderboards.

06 Deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete it at any time.

Delete from inside the app

Open Profile → Delete account, then confirm.

Or request deletion by email

Email support@auralis24.com from the address on your account and we will delete it within 30 days.

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, learning progress, streak and achievements, AI conversation history, and social connections. Anonymised records containing nothing that identifies you may be retained where required for accounting or fraud prevention.

Subscriptions are separate. Deleting your account does not cancel an active Google Play subscription. Cancel it in the Google Play Store to stop being billed.

07 Security

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS.
  • Passwords are stored only as salted hashes.
  • Database access is protected by row-level security, so your records are readable only by your own authenticated session.
  • AI provider keys are held server-side only and never included in the app.

No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

08 Children

SkillForge AI is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age required in your country if higher. We do not knowingly collect data from children under that age. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact support@auralis24.com and we will delete it.

09 Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. To exercise any of these, email support@auralis24.com.

10 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will update the “Last updated” date at the top and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
